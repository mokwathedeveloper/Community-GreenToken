# Community GreenToken Project Name and Branding

## 1. Project Name
**Community GreenToken**

- The name reflects the platform’s focus on **community-driven sustainable actions** and **token-based incentives**.
- Memorable, concise, and conveys social impact and gamification.

## 2. Branding Statement
- **Mission:** To empower communities to take sustainable actions while rewarding them transparently through blockchain technology.
- **Vision:** A world where eco-friendly behavior is incentivized, measurable, and socially recognized.

## 3. Brand Personality
- **Trustworthy:** Transparent token rewards, clear tracking of actions.
- **Friendly & Human:** Engaging microcopy, gamification, social interaction.
- **Professional & Modern:** Clean UI, responsive design, modern web technologies.
- **Inspirational:** Encourages positive environmental behavior through rewards and recognition.

## 4. Logo Direction
- **Concept:** A green, circular token symbol representing community collaboration and growth.
- **Typography:** Rounded, clean sans-serif font for approachability.
- **Colors:** Green primary (eco-focus), secondary accents (for actions/rewards), neutral background.
- **Style:** Minimalist with a slight 3D effect for token icon, flat design for digital adaptability.

## 5. Color Palette
- **Primary Color:** #2ECC71 (Green)
- **Secondary Color:** #27AE60 (Darker Green)
- **Accent Color:** #F1C40F (Yellow for reward highlights)
- **Background Color:** #FFFFFF (Clean and modern)
- **Text Color:** #333333 (Readable, neutral)

## 6. Typography
- **Headings:** Poppins Bold, 700 weight.
- **Body Text:** Poppins Regular, 400 weight.
- **CTA Buttons:** Poppins Medium, 500 weight.
- Maintain readability and accessibility.

## 7. Brand Voice
- **Tone:** Warm, encouraging, clear, and motivational.
- **Microcopy Examples:**
  - “Your eco-actions matter!”
  - “Earn tokens, make a difference.”
  - “Redeem your rewards for impact.”

## 8. Visual Style Guidelines
- **Iconography:** Simple, flat, friendly, consistent style.
- **Illustrations:** Community and sustainability-focused, inclusive, diverse characters.
- **Photography:** Real-world images of environmental actions, tree planting, recycling.
- **Layout:** Clean, modular, responsive, mobile-first.

## 9. Application & Assets
- All branding elements should be consistent across:
  - Web app interface
  - Landing page and marketing materials
  - Pitch decks and presentations
  - Social media assets

## 10. Branding Dos and Don'ts
- **Do:** Keep the green eco-friendly theme, consistent typography, and clear CTAs.
- **Don't:** Use generic icons, overly complex illustrations, or unrelated color palettes.

This document serves as the **primary reference for Community GreenToken branding** and project identity for the web application development and hackathon presentation.
